# LidGlass 应用封面与图标

使用内置 imagegen 生成，原始 PNG 为 `LidGlass.png`。构建时自动转换为 macOS 多尺寸 ICNS，供应用图标和控制窗口使用。

设计意图：半开的银色笔记本，屏幕顶部呈毛玻璃虚化，靠近底部铰链的位置保持清晰；深蓝圆角底板、青蓝光线、无文字。

## 原始提示词

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: final macOS app icon and square app cover for LidGlass, a MacBook hinge-angle progressive screen blur utility. Create ONE polished 1024x1024 square icon, no text, no lettering, no logos or watermarks. Center a beautifully simple partially open silver laptop, front three-quarter view with a clear lower hinge line, occupying about 72% of the square. Its glass display illustrates the actual function: a luminous cyan to indigo abstract desktop whose bottom near the hinge is crisp and whose upper region smoothly dissolves into soft frosted glass, strongest blur at the top. Keep laptop silhouette crisp, very few details, readable at tiny sizes, no keyboard keys or tiny UI widgets. Premium restrained 3D macOS utility aesthetic, soft studio reflections and subtle depth. Background is a single rounded-square dark navy tile with generous corner radius, subtle blue halo behind laptop, clean transparent outer corners outside tile. Balanced centered composition, no extra objects, no arrows, no badges, no mockup presentation sheet. Output a standalone finished icon.
